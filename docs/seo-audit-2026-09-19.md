**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Type:** Local business (HVAC contractor, Sarasota FL, service-area business)
**Follows:** `seo-geo-audit-2026-09-01.md`, whose schema, FAQ layer, llms.txt and headers are still in place.
**Measurement:** tracked by `~/seo-pulse` (adjustments `EA1`–`EA14`); baseline taken 2026-09-19.

## Diagnosis — why there are basically zero visits

The Sept 1 pass made the pages good. It didn't make them **findable**. Five facts, in order of impact:

1. **The business doesn't exist on Google Maps.** No Google Business Profile: `/api/reviews` answers
   `GOOGLE_PLACE_ID … not set`, and no Place ID exists anywhere. For "AC repair Sarasota" and every
   "… near me" search, Google shows the **map pack first** (3 businesses with reviews). Most HVAC calls
   come from there. Without a profile, EagleAir can't appear in it at all.
2. **All 15 city pages are orphans.** Not one page links to `venice.html`, `bradenton.html` or any other
   city page, not even the homepage (it links only the 7 service pages). They exist solely in
   `sitemap.xml`. Google treats a page nobody links to as unimportant, and usually won't index it.
3. **The city pages are ~75 % copies.** Each is the homepage template with a local block: of ~1,550
   words, only 21–26 % are unique to the page. All 15 share the homepage's H1 ("AC Out? We'll Be There
   Before You Break a Sweat."). Combined with (2), this is textbook doorway-page pattern.
4. **The domain is 6 months old** (registered 2026-03-20) with no backlinks, no reviews, no profiles
   (`sameAs` is empty, and the footer social icons are `href="#"`). "Eagle Air" is also a crowded name:
   Livermore CA (236 Yelp reviews), eagleairsa, eagleairusa, eagleaircv and more. Google needs
   Sarasota-specific proof to tell this EagleAir apart.
5. **The competition is decades deep.** Page 1 for *AC repair Sarasota FL*: Aqua (since 1974), Tri County
   Air (1977), All American (1985), D&D Air (30+ years), SRQ AC, Reliance. Head terms aren't winnable
   organically this year. The winnable ground is the map pack (after GBP + reviews), the smaller towns
   (Rotonda West, Deep Creek, Murdock, Nokomis, Osprey), and long-tail questions.

### Confirmed by Search Console, 2026-09-20

`seo-pulse` now reads the property directly. **Google has indexed 0 of the 25 URLs.** The coverage
states prove the diagnosis above rather than merely suggesting it:

| Coverage state | URLs | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| *URL is unknown to Google* | all 15 city pages + `air-conditioning`, `heating`, `emergency-repair`, `mini-split` | Google has never even seen these. For the city pages that is exactly what orphaning predicts (Diagnosis 2) |
| *Crawled – currently not indexed* | homepage, `ventilation`, `maintenance`, `installation`, … | Google fetched them and chose not to index — the usual verdict for a new domain with no authority and heavily templated pages (Diagnosis 3, 4) |

The sitemap was last read on 2026-09-12 (25 URLs, 0 errors), so the sitemap is fine — discovery is
not the whole problem, *selection* is. The 28-day window shows **67 non-brand impressions, 0 clicks**,
almost all on micro-volume queries the site happens to match (`ac repair myakka city`,
`air conditioner repair manasota`, `hvac the meadows`).

**This reorders the plan:** EA1 (internal links) and EA4 (de-duplication) are no longer "nice
structure" — they are the two things standing between these pages and the index. After they ship,
request indexing manually in Search Console for the homepage, the 7 service pages and the 5 biggest
city pages; that is the only way to push re-crawling (the URL Inspection API is read-only, and the
Indexing API does not cover ordinary pages).

## Findings

### Critical
1. **No Google Business Profile** (Diagnosis 1). Create it as a **service-area business** (address
   hidden), primary category *HVAC contractor*, secondary *Air conditioning repair service* and
   *Heating contractor*. Service areas = the 15 cities. Verification is usually by video. Then put the
   Place ID + a Places key in Vercel env, and the existing daily-cached `/api/reviews` shows real
   reviews with no code change.
2. **15 orphaned city pages** (Diagnosis 2). Fix: an "Areas we serve" block on the homepage (15 links
   grouped by county: Sarasota / Manatee / Charlotte) and the same list in the footer of every page;
   on each city page, links to the 2–3 neighbouring towns.
3. **Search Console is verified but unused.** *(Corrected 2026-09-20: the domain property is verified —
   DNS `google-site-verification` TXT record. The first version of this audit wrongly inferred the
   opposite from the absence of a verification tag in the repo.)* It is the fastest way to confirm
   Diagnosis 2 and 3: open **Indexing → Pages** and see how many of the 25 URLs are actually indexed.
   Submit the sitemap if it isn't, and add the `seo-pulse` service account so indexing is tracked.

### High
4. **City pages duplicate the homepage** (Diagnosis 3). Give each a city H1 ("AC Repair & HVAC Service
   in Venice, FL"), keep the unique local section + city FAQ as the body, and replace the copied
   homepage blocks (why-us bento, process, big testimonial area) with a short services grid linking to
   the service pages. Target ≥ 50 % unique text per page.
5. **Service-page H1s are bare labels** ("Air Conditioning", "Heating Services", "Installation"). The
   titles are good. Make the H1s say "AC Repair & Installation in Sarasota, FL" and so on.
6. **Florida contractor licence number missing.** Florida law requires the state registration or
   certification number in every advertisement, a website included (F.S. 489.119(5)(b)). It's also a
   top trust signal for homeowners and a requirement for Google Local Services Ads. Show it in the
   footer + `hasCredential` in schema. The LLC document number L24000364625 is not a licence.
7. **Dead links:** logo and footer "Home" are `href="#"` (should be `/`), three social icons and
   "Terms of Service" are `href="#"`. Link real profiles or remove the icons; create a Terms page or
   remove the link.
8. **Apex → www redirect is 307 (temporary).** Vercel → Domains → `eagleair-hvac.com` → 308.

### Medium
9. **Phone number is a 904 (Jacksonville) area code** for a Sarasota business. Customers and Google
   read local numbers as local presence. If a 941 number is possible (a forwarding number is fine), use
   it on the site, GBP and every citation. Consistency matters more than the area code, so decide
   before the citations go out.
10. **Citations:** Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, BBB, Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, the
    Sarasota chamber, all with an identical name/phone/URL. Every one becomes a `sameAs` link.
11. **Nobody behind the business.** "15 years' experience" with no named person. Add an About page:
    owner name, photo, licence, where the 15 years were earned (`Person` schema, `founder`).
12. **No dated, question-led content.** 3 guides, e.g. *Why is my AC leaking water?*, *R-410A phase-out:
    repair or replace in 2026?*, *Hurricane-season HVAC checklist for Sarasota*. Link each to its service page.
13. **Optional, paid:** Google Local Services Ads ("Google Guaranteed") put an HVAC business above the
    map pack within weeks of approval. It needs the licence + insurance. Worth it if organic results
    are too slow for the business.
